Ma Python va C++ da duoc thuc thi tren Linux trong qua trinh bien soan
va cho dau ra giong het nhau tren cac ca kiem thu (45, 0, 1, 60, abc;
tieu chi 1, 2, 3, 0, abc; n = 9, 7, 4, 1, 0, abc), ke ca ban ca nhan,

moi truong bien soan. Can chay xac nhan tren may lop truoc khi dung
Julia de danh gia. Chua co kiem thu thuc thi truc tiep tren Windows/macOS.
